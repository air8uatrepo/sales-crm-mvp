# Change statement

## Requested outcome

Build a first-phase CRM for sales staff to record and view customer information. Sales users can enter a customer's basic information (company name, company contact, cooperating buyer, BD email) and submit it; records start as draft and become submitted after submission. Sales users can view customer information. No login is included in the first phase.

## Confirmed behavior

- Users are sales staff.
- Sales staff can enter customer basic information: enterprise/company name, company contact details, cooperating buyer, and the BD's own email.
- Sales staff can submit a customer record.
- Record status is draft before submission and submitted after submission.
- Sales staff can view customer information.
- The first phase has no login.
- The target is a new application: Sales CRM MVP.

## Open business questions

- Can a draft be edited or deleted after submission, and is a submitted record immutable?
- Are customer records visible to every sales user, or only to the creator?
- Which specific contact fields make up "company contact" beyond the named ones?

## This change does not

No repository, branch, worktree, or implementation is selected during intake.
